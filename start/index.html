Show that your software is human-shaped.

The Human-Shaped Principles belong to anyone who builds by them, and there are two marks for saying so. Nobody approves either one. Each mark says something true about how your software was made, and you can use it as soon as it is true of yours.

  • Aligned with Human Shaped

    You have written down how your software lines up with each of the fifteen principles, in a file called HUMAN-SHAPED.md in your own repository.

  • Endorsed by Human Shaped

    Your software started from the Universal App Template, or you have written your commitment to the principles into your project. Either one is enough.

  1. Read the principles against your own software, and notice the ones it does not meet yet.
  2. Copy HUMAN-SHAPED.md into your repository.
  3. For each principle, say whether your software meets it, does not meet it yet, or does not apply, in a sentence or two of your own, with a link to evidence someone else can open. Your agent can help you find the evidence, and the sentences are yours to write.
  4. Show the mark that is true of your software, linked to that file.

The marks, for your README and your site.

Show a mark wherever people meet your software, in its README on GitHub or at the foot of its website. The mark links to your own HUMAN-SHAPED.md, so anyone who wonders what it means can read your answers and the evidence behind them, which is what makes a mark like this worth trusting. Please keep its colors and its shape as they are, because people only learn to recognize a mark when it looks the same everywhere they see it.

Paste this where you want the mark, and change the first address to your own file. It follows the reader's light or dark setting on GitHub and on most websites. For the endorsed mark, change aligned to endorsed in both image addresses and in the alt text.
