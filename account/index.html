Your account

Sign in with GitHub.

Your GitHub account is where your app lives and where your cohort talks, so it is also how you sign in here. This site keeps only what a cohort needs, and everything you build stays in your own account.

Checking whether you are signed in…

GitHub will ask whether to share your public profile with Human Shaped. That is all we ask for, and you can take it back at any time from your GitHub settings.

You can also sign in with Google. Cohorts work through GitHub, so the first time, you will be asked to link your GitHub account too.

Once you are signed in, this window hands your sign-in back to the Human Shaped panel in Google Meet and closes.

Your cohorts

Cohorts you can join

Help a new cohort as a mentor

You hold the credential, so you can join a cohort that is open or running as a mentor. You are in it like everyone else, with no app of your own to finish, there to show how you worked, answer what you can, and give feedback when people ask for it. Everyone in the cohort can see that you are a mentor, and its teachers still give the feedback that counts.

Your credential

What your teachers recorded when your cohort finished. Once it is signed, its page holds the credential itself, which you can share by its link, download for a credential wallet that reads Open Badges 3.0, or have anyone check.

Notes from your teachers

These are yours to keep after a cohort ends or you leave it, and yours to remove. Only you and the cohort's teachers can read them.

Your path

What you marked on the stages of the path. Only you can see it, and nothing here is counted or compared.

Your own app

For an app you are building on your own, outside any cohort. It stays yours alone until you choose to show it, and then the apps page shows its name, repository, and address, with a page that follows its work, and never anything about you.

How your teachers can reach you

A teacher who has not seen you in a while may want to write to you privately. Until you choose otherwise, their words wait for you here and on your cohort page. Both choices below are off until you turn them on, and you can turn them off at any time.

That second email comes from meet@humanshaped.org and says only that something is waiting, never the words themselves.

Linked accounts

The ways you can sign in to this one account. You can add or take one away, but never the last.

    Your data

    This site keeps your GitHub name and picture, which cohorts you are in, whatever you choose to share with a cohort, your own marks on the path, your own app if you add one, the notes your teachers send you, and, only if you give it above, the address your teachers may use. Leaving a cohort removes what you shared there but keeps your notes, which you can remove here. Deleting your account removes everything.

    Delete your account?

    This removes your profile, your place in every cohort, and everything you shared on this site. Your GitHub account and your apps are not touched.

    Leave this cohort?

    What you shared with this cohort will be removed, and your classmates will no longer see your app here. Your app itself stays yours, on GitHub.
