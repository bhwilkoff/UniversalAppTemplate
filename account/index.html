Cohorts you can join

Your data

This site keeps your GitHub name and picture, which cohorts you are in, and whatever you choose to share with a cohort. Leaving a cohort removes what you shared there, and deleting your account removes everything.

Delete your account?

This removes your profile, your place in every cohort, and everything you shared on this site. Your GitHub account and your apps are not touched.

Leave this cohort?

What you shared with this cohort will be removed, and your classmates will no longer see your app here. Your app itself stays yours, on GitHub.
