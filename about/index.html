About

Human Shaped exists so that the future keeps being shaped by humans.

When anyone can build their own apps with AI, it matters even more which apps they choose to build, and how they choose to build them.

What Human Shaped is

  • A place to learn by building

    Free 5-week cohorts, where each person builds one real app for a human-shaped problem, beside a small group doing the same.

  • A hub for software made for people

    Every app built this way, with its work in the open, for anyone building software for people instead of profit.

  • A movement anyone can start

    Meetups, hackathons, and marks for declaring your own software human-shaped, wherever you are.

The ideas, the method, and the tools are free. Anyone can teach a cohort, and what you build stays yours. Every app built this way is measured against the same fifteen principles.

Who makes it

Human Shaped is made by Ben Wilkoff and offered by Learning is Change, Inc. It grew out of building real apps with AI agents, and those lessons live in the Universal App Template, free for anyone to copy. The people and tools behind this site are credited on the help page.
