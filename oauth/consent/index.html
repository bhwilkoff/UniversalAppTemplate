Your agent

Connect your AI agent.

Your own Claude or Gemini can know what your cohort is working on, so that when you ask it for help, it starts from this week's challenge and your own work rather than from nothing. It reads as you, and it can only read.

Checking what is being asked…

Sign in with the same GitHub account you use for your cohort, and then you can decide.

Your agent wants to read your cohorts as you.

Signed in as . It will send you back to .

What it can read

  • The cohorts you are in or teach
  • This week's challenge and the stages it covers
  • The next session's time, agenda, and Meet link
  • During a session, your place in the queue to show your work, your teacher's open questions, and your own answers
  • Your group, with each partner's GitHub name, app, and repository
  • Your own app, what you have shared, and the feedback on it
  • The method itself, from the Universal App Template

What it can never do

  • Share anything with your cohort, or give feedback
  • Answer your teacher's questions, or add to the queue, for you
  • Change, join, leave, or delete anything
  • See anyone's email address, including yours
  • Read what your classmates shared outside your group

What your agent says to you stays with you. If you want your cohort to see it, you share it yourself, on your cohort page. To disconnect it later, remove the Human Shaped connector in your agent's settings.
