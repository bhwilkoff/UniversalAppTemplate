Asking to teach

People who would like to lead a cohort, oldest first. Approving one makes them a teacher here, able to make cohorts of their own, and whatever you write in the answer is what they will read.

Waiting to be signed

Credentials the teachers of any cohort have recorded, oldest first. Copy one, run node tools/credential/sign-waiting.mjs in the site folder on the computer that holds the key, and paste what it puts back on your clipboard here. The page checks the signature before it saves anything.

The cohort's conversation on GitHub

A private repository in the humanshaped organization with Discussions on, and a secret team that can reach it. People are added to the team when they open the conversation from their cohort page. The hub makes both when you press "Make the cohort’s private repository and team on GitHub" under Before the first session, so fill these in only for a repository and team made by hand.

Open the live page Every rehearsal room now

In the call itself, the live page in a tab beside Meet does everything a teacher needs. The Human Shaped add-on puts the same things in Meet’s own side panel, and it works for meet@humanshaped.org only until it is listed: who can use it, and how it is installed.

Before the first session

What this cohort needs before people arrive. Each step checks itself off when the hub can see it is done, and the teaching guide says more about each one.

      Sessions

      Each week’s run of show is the plan you will run in Meet: its scenes in order, each with its minutes and what the main stage shows. Open one under its week to start from the six parts, copy last week’s, and change anything.

      Question bank

      The questions you prepare before class: choose one, choose any, a scale, a few words, put in order, or in their own words. Ask any of them in the moment from the Meet add-on or the live page, or plan one into a question scene. Students see a question only when you ask it, and what you ask is a copy, so changing it in class leaves the bank as it was.

      The lesson this cohort sends back

      In the last week, the cohort chooses together one thing it learned that the next builder should not have to learn again: a prompt that worked, a step that broke, or a correction that kept coming up. Send it to the template as an issue, and credit the people who learned it only if each of them said yes. Everything on GitHub is public, so leave out anything private about anyone.

      Teachers

      A cohort can have more than one teacher, and a new teacher leads best after co-leading a session of someone else's cohort first. Anyone who teaches here can be added, and a co-teacher sees and does everything you do on this page.

        Their apps on humanshaped.org

        Groups

        Trios, because a trio still has two people to show when one is away. Everyone can see their own group and what it is for, and you can change them whenever the cohort needs it. Each group has its own Meet room for the group part of every session, which meet@ makes when you ask it to set up the calls, and its members see it on their cohort page and the live page.
