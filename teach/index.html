Run a cohort.

A cohort is something you make here rather than something written into the site, so every detail of it (its dates, its weekly sessions, its groups, and what each week asks of people) is yours to set and to change as the cohort goes.

People who would like to lead a cohort, oldest first. Approving one makes them a teacher here, able to make cohorts of their own, and whatever you write in the answer is what they will read.

      Groups

      Pairs, trios, or small groups. Everyone can see their own group and what it is for, and you can change them whenever the cohort needs it. Each group can also have its own Meet link for the group part of every session (the Meet script can make one for each), which its members see on their cohort page and the session page.
