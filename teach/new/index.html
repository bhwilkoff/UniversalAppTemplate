This page is for teachers. Ask to teach a cohort, and once you are approved it opens for you.

Each answer shapes how the cohort runs: who it is for, when it meets, how many people can join, and who can see it. You can change any of them later, except its address.

1What will people call it?

Its name on the cohorts page and at the top of its own page.

Its address: humanshaped.org/cohort/?c=…

2Who is it for, and what will they build?

A sentence or two in your own words, shown under its name. Say who you hope will join and the kind of problem they might bring.

3When does week 1 begin?

Cohort Prep is the week before it, and the cohort runs for 5 weeks from here.

4When does the class meet each week?

One live session a week. The first is on the first of these days on or after week 1 begins, and everyone sees the time in their own time zone too.

5How many people can join?

Everyone works in a group of three that stays together, so a multiple of three keeps everyone in a group. Leave it empty for no limit.

6Where does it stand?
7Who can read its conversation?

The cohort talks in Discussions in its own private repository on GitHub, which the hub makes for you under Before the first session.
