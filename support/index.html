Support

Ask where the answer helps the next person.

Help with the site, a cohort, or the Human Shaped add-on for Google Meet, and the things you can do yourself.

Written by Claude, awaiting Ben's review.

Where to ask.

I want help here to come from a person who knows the course, so the best place to ask is wherever your question will help the next person too.

  • If you are in a cohort, ask your teacher, or post in your cohort's conversation, which is linked from your cohort page.
  • For anything about the site, the add-on, or the method, ask in the discussions on GitHub.
  • For something private, such as removing information about you, write to meet@humanshaped.org.
  • If you found a bug in the template, open an issue on its repository.

The add-on for Google Meet.

Open Human Shaped from Meet's Activities button. The side panel finds the cohort from the call's link, so the call's link has to be the one on the session's page (a teacher sets it on the teaching page). If the panel cannot sign you in, it offers a small window to sign in with GitHub, and if neither works, a link to the session page in a new tab, which has everything the panel has.

The panel asks Google for no permissions and reads only the meeting code, which the privacy page explains.

What you can do yourself.

From your account page you can sign out, leave a cohort, remove notes from your teachers, and delete your account. From your cohort page you can take your app off the public apps page.
