The path

Nine stages, from a why to every platform.

The path is the course, and it is the same path whether you walk it alone with the template or with a cohort. Each stage opens with something that really happened while an app was being built this way, says what the stage is for, and ends with a "Be ready to..." line, which is what you bring back and show another person.

Every round of every stage makes the same three moves: write, play, and publish. A cohort walks all nine stages in about five weeks, and how a cohort works says what each week asks of you.

The stages

  1. ·Getting set upA GitHub account, your own copy, an agent, and your app's address on your phone.
  2. 00Why we buildAn agent keeps only the values you write down.
  3. 01The first prototypeOne platform, real data, this week.
  4. 02The shape of an appSame verb, native idiom.
  5. 03Going nativeThe floor is a floor, not a ceiling.
  6. 04Seeing it workThe agent is never the tester.
  7. 05ShippingA feature nobody can install is unfinished.
  8. 06Keeping it runningA confident zero is worse than no dashboard.
  9. 07Raising the ceilingRaise the ceiling without raising the floor.
  10. 08Working with AIThe agent remembers only what the repository remembers.
  11. Talking to your agentThe prompt is the work, so this page gathers the moves that work with an agent, alongside every stage.
  12. Showing your workExplain one decision, ask three questions, and find feedback with or without a cohort.

Every stage is a set of conversations with your agent. Connect it to Human Shaped and it can read your cohort's week, or your own app if you are building alone, and review your work the way the method does.
