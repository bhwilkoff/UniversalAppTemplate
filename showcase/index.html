    Being a good guest.

    Everyone in the room has spent five weeks building something for people rather than for profit, and tonight they show it to strangers for the first time. The cohort keeps four rules, and they hold for guests too: no correcting what does not matter, no acting surprised, no taking over, and no small digs at who someone is.

    You are welcome to ask one question after someone shows their app. Ask about a decision, what it was like to use, or what comes next, and leave the critique of the code to the people who wrote it. The cohortHow we treat one another says the rest.rsquo;s own agreement says the rest.
