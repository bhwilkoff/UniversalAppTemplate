# The cohort page's design

Binding for `/cohort/`, like SESSION-DESIGN.md and ADDON-DESIGN.md. Fix this file first, then the page.

Ben, November 6, 2026, of Waiting for you: "What are these?" and "Is there anything else we can do to further clean up the Cohort pages so that they are more in line with our design as well as the content/information we are trying to hone in on within our cohort?"

The cohort's home answers one question: what is next, and what does it need from me?

## The shape

```
Minerva University ... (a modest title)   Leader
This week  Sessions  Conversation  People  Files  Settings

+---------------------------------------------------+
| Next                                [Join the call]|
| Week 2, Wednesday at 1:00 PM (links to its page)  |
| [=====|==|=========|====|===|=======|==]           |
| What to do before it                               |
+---------------------------------------------------+
Waiting for you      (only when something is)
Shared with the cohort
```

| Tab | Holds |
|---|---|
| This week | The next session and what it asks, what waits for you, and what people have shared. |
| Sessions | The sessions, and nothing else. What came of one is on its own page. A weekly cohort's leader makes them from the schedule; a cohort with sessions you set adds them one at a time with Add a session, which opens the new session's page to name and date it. |
| Conversation | The cohort's conversation, then its exchanges. |
| People | Each learner as a card, with their app, what they have done, and a place to write to them; then Try my app, groups, leaders, apps, and credentials. |
| Files, Your work, Settings | As they were. |

## Rules

1. A row in Waiting for you is something only you can act on, with a real count, and it opens the exact thing. Nothing waiting means no section.
2. Say only what the site knows. A person is never described by what the site did not record: it cannot see who was in a call, so it never says who was absent.
3. Facts about a learner are facts (answered the pre-work, showed their app), by name, and never a score or a comparison.
4. The title introduces the page. It never fills the first screen.
5. The next session leads, whatever week it is.
6. Old addresses keep working: `?t=asks` opens People, and `?t=exchanges` opens Conversation.
