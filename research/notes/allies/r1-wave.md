## 1. Communities, courses, and cohorts teaching people to build with AI agents

### Anthropic education and nonprofit programs
- **Claude Corps** (https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-corps). A $150M fellowship placing about 1,000 early-career fellows inside US nonprofits for a year to help them use Claude. First cohort of 100 starts October 2026; later cohorts February 2027 and August 2027; fellow applications for those are rolling; **nonprofits can apply now to host for any start date**. Fellows: over 18, fewer than two years of full-time work, US work authorization. **Checked (2026-10-08).** Run with CodePath and Social Finance; hosts include Code for America, Goodwill, YMCA; host grant reported at $10,000. **Search.**
  - *Aligns:* adoption inside mission-driven organizations. *Tension:* it is tool adoption by one vendor; Human Shaped is model-agnostic in spirit (Claude or Gemini).
  - *Join in:* Claude Corps fellows are early-career people who will need to learn to build for their host nonprofits. A Human Shaped cohort is a natural free resource to offer them. Reach CodePath (employer of record) and ask whether fellows can join a cohort.
- **AI Fluency for Nonprofits** (https://anthropic.skilljar.com/ai-fluency-for-nonprofits). Free course built with GivingTuesday on the "4D" framework (Delegation, Description, Discernment, Diligence). **Search.** *Aligns:* free, nonprofit focus. *Gap:* fluency, not building. *Join in:* cite it as a prerequisite or companion; GivingTuesday (December 1, 2026, the Tuesday after US Thanksgiving, **From memory**) is a natural moment for a cohort announcement.
- **Claude for Teachers + AI Fluency for K-12 Teachers.** Launched July 14, 2026: free premium Claude for verified US K-12 teachers who sign up by June 30, 2027; a free, model-agnostic, Creative Commons course built with Teach For America, and a train-the-trainer module with AFT. **Search** (multiple secondary outlets agree; Anthropic's page not opened). District version reported as later added (Sept 24, 2026 coverage), unconfirmed.
  - *Aligns strongly:* teachers now have free agent access; the CC license means Human Shaped can link to or adapt it. This removes the cost barrier for Ben's core educator audience. *Join in:* a "teachers' cohort" timed to this window; tell teachers how to get Claude for Teachers on the Cohort Prep page.
- **Claude Campus / Claude Builder Clubs** (https://claude.com/programs/campus). Paid student ambassadors run builder clubs, hackathons, demo nights. 2026-27 cohort applications reportedly closed September 12, 2026. **Search.** *Join in:* builder clubs need curricula for non-CS members; offer the template and a cohort to club leaders.

### Google and Kaggle
- **AI Agents Intensive, Vibe Coding edition** (https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/kaggle-genai-intensive-course-vibe-coding-june-2026/). Free five-day online course, June 15 to 19, 2026; earlier edition reached over 1.5 million learners. **Checked (2026-10-08).** *Conflicts:* scale over care, five days not five weeks, no values layer. *Use:* evidence of demand; Human Shaped is the slower, human counterpart.

### University libraries (a quiet, aligned network)
- **Notre Dame Hesburgh Libraries, "Vibe Coding 101"**, Oct 16, 2026, 1 to 3 pm; **CUNY AI Lab Fall 2026** vibe coding track with a "Bring Your Own Project Clinic" Oct 13 and drop-ins Oct 29, Nov 17, Dec 3; **UVA Library** (Sept 15 workshop plus consultations); **Villanova Falvey Library** (June 11). **Search.**
  - *Aligns:* free, non-coders, ethics alongside building; libraries are civic and trusted. *Gap:* single sessions, no cohort, no shipping. *Join in:* offer the five-week arc as the "what next" after a one-off workshop; CUNY's drop-in clinics are the closest model to Human Shaped's. Pitch librarians as cohort teachers.

### Paid cohorts (the commercial neighbors)
- **Maven**: "Claude Code for Non-Technical Founders and Creators" (Oct 3 to Nov 9, 2026), "Vibe Coding Academy: Ship Your First SaaS" (Sept 14 to Nov 2, 2026, 10 seats), "Become an AI-Native Builder" ($2,500, Nov 9 to Dec 9, 2026), others. **Search.**
  - *Conflicts:* priced, aimed at founders, profit and SaaS framing. This is the contrast that makes "free, for people not profit" legible. Do not position against them by name; just state what Human Shaped is.
- **Coursera / Vanderbilt "Build Anything with AI: No Code Required"** and "Building Apps and AI Agents with Claude Code". **Search.** Self-paced, not cohort.

### Thoughtful learning communities
- **Recurse Center, "Developing our position on AI"** (https://www.recurse.com/blog/191-developing-our-position-on-ai), July 23, 2025, by Nick Bergson-Shilcock. Conclusions: use AI to extend your abilities without giving up agency; you must understand what you build; LLMs can pull people away from asking each other for help. **Checked (2026-10-08).**
  - *Aligns deeply* with "learning counts as success" and the community-over-tool stance. *Join in:* cite it; invite RC alumni as cohort teachers.
- **Hack Club** (https://hackclub.com). Teen "You Ship, We Ship" programs. No AI-specific 2026 program found. **Search.** *Note:* audience under 18; Human Shaped cohorts likely adult; a future youth track would need its own design.
- **Processing Foundation / p5.js**. No formal AI policy; a p5.js "Critical AI" tutorial teaches using genAI to write code while explaining why critical perspectives matter. Processing Community Day events worldwide in October 2026 for Processing's 25th anniversary. **Search.** *Aligns:* access, beginners, critical literacy. *Join in:* a local Processing Community Day this month is a place to meet creative coders who care.
- **Ink & Switch, "Malleable Software"** essay (June 2025; Geoffrey Litt, Josh Horowitz, Peter van Hardenberg, Todd Matthews). Argues AI coding has potential but is not sufficient on its own for a new era of personal software. **Search.** *Aligns:* builder agency, people shaping their own tools.

### Practitioners showing the way
- **Francis Irving, "Vibe coding personal apps in mid-2026"** (https://www.flourish.org/2026/08/personal-apps/). Three personal local-first apps built with Claude in a month (including a face-memory aid because he is face blind); writes plainly that he has not reviewed the code closely and does not feel he owns it. **Checked (2026-10-08).** *Aligns:* real human problem, people own their data (local-first, self-hosted). His unease about ownership is exactly what "never ship a first draft" and the review skill answer. *Join in:* he is a good person to invite to show work or speak.
- **Kevin Roose, "software for one"** (NYT). **Search** (secondary roundup only).

## 3. Manifestos, pledges, declarations, and the backlash

### Aligned declarations
- **The Resonant Computing Manifesto** (https://resonantcomputing.org/). Launched Dec 5, 2025 at WIRED's Big Interview; lead instigator Alex Komoroske, with Maggie Appleton, Sam Arbesman, and others; Simon Willison and Tim O'Reilly are early signers; later signatories include Hank Green and Esther Dyson. Five principles: Private, Dedicated, Plural, Adaptable, Prosocial. Signing is through a Google Form; there is a shared "theses" document open for contributions and a Resonant Computing Lab. **Checked (2026-10-08)** for principles and signing; signatory names and a "1,122 signatories" count are **Search**. An NYC "Resonant Computing Collective" event ran on Luma (**Search**, date not captured).
  - *Aligns closely:* "Dedicated" and "Private" match "more possible not more profit" and "people own their data". *Difference:* it speaks to professional technologists; Human Shaped teaches ordinary people to build. *Join in:* Ben can sign; contribute a thesis about teaching non-programmers; invite the authors to see the cohort apps. This is the most natural ally for the "declare your software human-shaped" toolkit.
- **Ink & Switch "Malleable Software"** (see section 1). Intellectual neighbor.

### Human-made labels (adjacent, partly hostile)
- **Lee Phillips, "Human Made: the Pledge"** (https://lee-phillips.org/humanmade/). Badge saying all "text, images, music, and computer code" on a site is hand-crafted, with no LLM output; AI may aid research only. Undated. **Checked (2026-10-08).**
  - *Conflicts:* a Human Shaped app built with an agent could never carry this badge. Human Shaped must not borrow the "human made" phrase; "human-shaped" is about whom software serves and how it was judged, not who typed it. Worth saying so plainly on the declare page.
- **Matt Kindt "100% Human-Made" mark** (April 9, 2026, CC license, honor system); **no-AI labels** broadly ("human-made", "proudly human", "AI-free"); **Spotify "Verified by Spotify"** withheld from AI-generated music; **major labels' July 2026 "substantially human made" chart rules**; **Aerie's October 2025 pledge** on no AI-generated bodies. **Search.**
  - *Lesson:* the public now hears "human" next to "AI" as "no AI". Human Shaped's mark needs a one-line explanation wherever it appears: made with AI agents, judged and owned by people, for people.

### Backlash Human Shaped must understand
- **"An open letter from educators who refuse the call to adopt GenAI in education"** (https://openletter.earth/an-open-letter-from-educators-who-refuse-the-call-to-adopt-genai-in-education-cb4aee75). July 6, 2025. Signatories (e.g. Melanie Dusseau, Miriam Reynoldson) commit not to use GenAI for grading, feedback, or course design, not to train students to use it to replace their own intellectual work, and to protect the right to refuse. 1,458 signatures, 1,295 verified. **Checked (2026-10-08).**
  - *Conflicts and overlaps:* Ben's own audience is educators, and some of them signed this. Human Shaped already agrees with several of its points: AI feedback never stands in for the teacher's, the student decides what to share, and learning counts. Naming that common ground respectfully, and honoring the right to refuse, is better than arguing.
- **CCCC resolution on the right to refuse GenAI** (March 2026, reported by Inside Higher Ed), framed as academic freedom. **Search.** Writing faculty are an organized, articulate bloc.
- **Local petitions** such as "Stop AI in Malden Schools" asking for no-penalty opt-outs. **Search.**
- **Leon Furze, "The Right to Resist"** (July 28, 2025), a sympathetic non-signer arguing the letter is about choice, not a ban. **Search.** Furze is a useful bridge voice.
- **Brian Merchant, Blood in the Machine** (https://www.bloodinthemachine.com/): newsletter and book reframing the Luddites as opposing how owners used machines, not machines themselves; October 2026 post interviewed Emily M. Bender. **Search.** *Overlap:* "more possible not more profit" is a Luddite-compatible position; Human Shaped is about who controls the tool and whom it serves.
- **Ed Zitron (Where's Your Ed At), 404 Media, Audrey Watters (Second Breakfast)**: prominent AI-skeptic writers. **From memory.** Their readers will test any claim Human Shaped makes; keep claims modest and grounded.

## 4. Funders, fellowships, and grants

**Important precondition:** most of these require a US 501(c)(3) or a fiscal sponsor. Whether Learning is Change, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) was not checked; if not, a fiscal sponsor is the first step.

- **Humanity AI $10M Open Call** (https://humanityai.ai/open-call/). Pooled fund of Doris Duke, Ford, Lumina, Kapor, MacArthur, Mellon, Mozilla, Omidyar, Packard, and Siegel (funder list **Search**). **Initial applications due Oct 21, 2026, 4:59 pm PT / 7:59 pm ET.** Lead must be a US 501(c)(3) (or public state university or tribal government); individuals apply through a fiscal sponsor. Tiers: $75K to $150K, $150K to $500K, $500K to $1M; projects up to three years. Issue areas: education, labor and economy, humanities/arts/culture. Phase 2 notices December 2026; awards early spring 2027. Prefers open-weight models where AI is central. **Ruled out: "AI literacy efforts focused on teaching tool skills" and "AI adoption efforts focused on promoting specific tools."** **Checked (2026-10-08).**
  - *Fit:* plausible only if framed as community-led building "on community terms" (educators shaping AI around students' interests; people owning their data), not as teaching Claude Code. The explicit exclusions are a real risk for a course that names Claude and Gemini. Most time-sensitive item here.
- **MIT Solve 2027 Global Learning Challenge** (https://solve.mit.edu/challenges/2027-global-learning-challenge). "How can we close global education gaps while improving outcomes for every learner?" **Deadline Nov 2, 2026, 12:00 pm ET.** Prototype stage or later. All Solvers get a $10K grant; average $40K; extra prizes include the **AI for Humanity Prize** (up to $150K, Patrick J. McGovern Foundation) and the **Citizens Workforce Innovation Prize** ($100K, US team). **Checked (2026-10-08).** *Fit:* good; Human Shaped is past concept stage once its first cohort runs. Org type not restricted on the page.
- **OpenAI Foundation People-First AI Fund**: 2026 round ($50M to 163 orgs) closed July 15, 2026. Unrestricted grants. **Search** (Foundation pages appeared in results, not opened). *Watch* for a 2027 round.
- **Patrick J. McGovern Foundation**: largely relationship-based, issues targeted RFPs. **Search.** Route in through the MIT Solve prize.
- **Mozilla Foundation**: Democracy x AI cohort closed March 16, 2026; Responsible Computing Challenge (with Mellon) is for US universities. **Search.** Mozilla is also in Humanity AI.
- **Knight Foundation**: Knight Cities Challenge closed April 30, 2026. **Search.**
- **Siegel Family Endowment**: no standalone open call found; funds via Humanity AI. **Search.**
- **NLnet NGI Zero Commons Fund**: final call closed June 1, 2026 (page also shows a possibly stale Nov 3 date). EU-oriented open-source code funding; weak fit. **Search.**
- **Google.org**: no confirmed open AI-education call found for late 2026. **Search.**
- **Alfred P. Sloan, Public Understanding of Science and Technology**: funds media, books, podcasts; possible fit only for a documentary or book about the movement. **Search.**
- **Hewlett Foundation OER**: no 2026 AI-and-open-education call found. **Search.** Human Shaped's free, CC-style materials are OER in spirit; worth a relationship conversation.
