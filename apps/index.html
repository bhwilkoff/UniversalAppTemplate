Built this way

Four apps came before the method.

Before I asked anyone to build like this, I built four apps this way myself, each with an AI agent and each for a problem I cared about, and the template you will start from is what they taught me. Every one of them can be opened and used today.

The four apps

  • Archive Watch

    "A cinematheque for the Internet Archive. Free, public domain, no account."

    Started April 17, 2026, and now on Apple TV, iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android, Google TV, Fire TV, Fire tablets, Roku, and the web, with 4,306 commits by the end of September.

    How it was built, month by month

    It gave the template most of what it knows about shared data, TV platforms, and testing on real devices, and the rule that only essential words belong on screen.

    • Follow its work
    • archivewatch.org
    • App Store
    • Google Play
    • Amazon Appstore
    • Roku

  • Tidbits Trivia

    "Trivia from the whole of Wikipedia. Play solo, keep a daily streak, or pass the phone."

    First built June 16, 2026, and now on the web, Apple's platforms, Android, and Windows through the Microsoft Store.

    It taught the template how to play together across platforms, and the rule that a computer opponent is always labeled as one.

    • Follow its work
    • tidbitstrivia.com
    • App Store
    • Google Play

  • Bsky Dreams

    "Built for people who want to think more, not less."

    A way to read Bluesky, on the web and the iPhone.

    It gave the template a feed ranked by the reader's own values rather than by engagement, and a reader mode for the links people share.

    • Follow its work
    • bskydreams.com
    • App Store

  • BOBA Playbook

    "The definitive companion app for the Bo Jackson Battle Arena trading card game."

    On the web and Android, and no longer in active development.

    BOBA Playbook was made independently, and not by the makers of the game.

    It is where the four questions every feature has to answer came from, and it stands as the proof that this method holds for large databases, huge numbers of files, camera scanning, 3D on iOS, and pricing.

    • Follow its work
    • bobaplaybook.com
    • Google Play

    More software built this way

      Yours

      Your app belongs beside these.

      When the first cohort begins, every app its people build will appear here beside these four, with a page that follows it as it grows. Apps made from the template are considered too, and so are apps built another way by people who hold themselves to the principles, and I read every one of them myself.

      If you built yours another way and hold it to the same principles, you can ask to have it listed, and the whole directory lives in the open at github.com/humanshaped/directory.

      If you are building on your own, outside any cohort, you can sign in and add your app on your account page, where it stays yours alone until you choose to show it here, with a page that follows its work like every other app on this page.

      If you want to start one now, the template is free to copy, and stage 00 is where every app begins.
