Human-shaped software

Build apps with an AI agent that leave people more capable, not more passive.

Some problems are computer-shaped. You can hand them to the window and be done. Learning, community and relationships are not, because they only happen when a person does the work.

Human-shaped software is built around those parts rather than over them. It takes the mechanical work off people's hands and leaves them the learning.

"Computer-shaped problems" comes from a Bluesky post.

Two shapes of problem

Not stages. Laps.

Three moves, every round

  1. 1
    Write it down.

    An agent keeps only the values you write down, so they live where it reads them.

  2. 2
    Prove it.

    The agent is never the tester. "It works" points at something a person can look at.

  3. 3
    Live with it.

    Use the app every day, with the people it is for. That is where the next feature, and the next value, come from.

Each lap of each stage makes all three.

Free. Anyone can teach one.

Five weeks, with other people

Each person builds one real app of their own, for people they can name. One live session a week, and every week ends with something to show at least two others.

Apps built this way

  • Archive Watch

    since April 17, 2026
    A cinematheque for the Internet Archive. Free, public domain, no account.

    Apple TV, iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android, Google TV, Fire TV, Roku, the web

    Gave the method the shared data plane, the TV platforms, and "only essential words on screen."

  • Tidbits Trivia

    Tidbits turns the whole of Wikipedia into a trivia game.

    The web, Apple platforms, Android, Windows

    Gave multiplayer across platforms, and the rule that a computer opponent is labeled as one.

  • Bsky Dreams

    Bsky Dreams is built for people who want to think more, not less.

    The web, iPhone

    Gave values-based feed ranking and reader mode.

  • BOBA Playbook

    not in active development
    The definitive companion app for the Bo Jackson Battle Arena trading card game.

    The web, Android

    The proof that this method holds for large databases, camera scanning, 3D on iOS and pricing models.
