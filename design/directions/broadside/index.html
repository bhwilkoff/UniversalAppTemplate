Human-shaped software

Build apps with an AI agent that leave people more capable, not more passive.

Some problems are computer-shaped. You can hand them to the window and be done. Learning, community and relationships are not, because they only happen when a person does the work.

Human-shaped software is built around those parts rather than over them. It takes the mechanical work off people's hands and leaves them the learning.

Computer-shaped

A blurb for ten thousand films

Cheap for a model. Left alone, the agent reaches for it, and it is not wrong to: nobody told it otherwise.

Human-shaped

Notes written by people, not a model

Archive Watch pulls each film's notes from the people who uploaded and reviewed it on archive.org. Slower, a pipeline to keep running, and the words stay theirs.

"Computer-shaped problems" is credited to the Bluesky post that started it.

No. 1The method

Three moves, every round

  1. 1

    Write it down

    An agent keeps only the values you write down, so they live where it reads them.

  2. 2

    Prove it

    The agent is never the tester. "It works" points at something a person can look at.

  3. 3

    Live with it

    Use the app every day, with the people it is for. That is where the next feature, and the next value, come from.

No. 2Cohorts

Five weeks. One real app.

Each person builds one app of their own, for people they can name. One live session a week. Every week ends with something to show at least two others.

Cohorts are free, and anyone can learn to teach one.
