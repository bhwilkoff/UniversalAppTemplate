Human-shaped software

Build apps with an AI agent that leave people more capable, not more passive.

Some problems are computer-shaped. You can hand them to the window and be done. Learning, community and relationships are not, because they only happen when a person does the work.

Human-shaped software is built around those parts rather than over them. It takes the mechanical work off people's hands and leaves them the learning.

Two shapes of problem

Computer-shaped

A blurb for ten thousand films. Cheap to hand to the window.

Human-shaped

Each film's notes, from the people who uploaded and reviewed it.

"Computer-shaped problems" comes from a Bluesky post.

Three moves, every round

not stages, laps
  1. 1

    Write it down

    An agent keeps only the values you write down, so they live where it reads them.

  2. 2

    Prove it

    The agent is never the tester. "It works" points at something a person can look at.

  3. 3

    Live with it

    Use the app every day, with the people it is for. That is where the next feature, and the next value, come from.

Five weeks, side by side

Each person builds one real app of their own, for people they can name. Partners see one another's work as it grows, and every week ends with something to show at least two others.

Cohorts are free. Anyone can learn to teach one.
