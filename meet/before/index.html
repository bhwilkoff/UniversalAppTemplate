Try it before your first session.

Five minutes in a Google Meet™ call of your own is enough to open Human Shaped, see its main stage, and learn the one move that brings the stage back if it closes, so that when your cohort meets, your attention can go to the people and not to the panel.

Add it, once.

Install Human Shaped from the Google Workspace Marketplace™ with the Google account you join calls with. Meet runs add-ons only in a computer's browser, so do this, and everything below, from a computer.

Open it in a call of your own.

  1. Start a call of your own in a new tab. No one else needs to join it.
  2. In the call, press Activities at the bottom right, then Add-ons, then Human Shaped. Its panel opens at the side, and you may be asked to sign in.
  3. Since this call is not a cohort's, the panel offers Start a session. Press it, then open the main stage, and the stage fills the middle of the call with a welcome.

When the panel and the stage are both showing, everything a session needs is working on your computer. You can end the call; nothing from it is kept.

The one move to remember.

In a session, your leader opens the main stage for everyone, and sometimes, to fix something or start again, they close it and open it once more. When that happens, Meet shows a notice at the top for a few seconds and then leaves only a small icon at the top right of the call. Press that icon, then Join activity, and your stage and panel come back where the session is.

Closing the side panel does not leave the session: the stage keeps going, and Activities, then Add-ons, then Human Shaped opens the panel again.

If something does not open.

Some schools and workplaces turn add-ons off for their accounts, and if Human Shaped is not under Add-ons, join with a personal Google account instead. If it still will not open, write to your leaders from your cohort's page before the session, and they can help you there rather than during the hour.
