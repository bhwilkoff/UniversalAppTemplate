Rent control, modelled with utility

  • A city of 100 rental flats. Demand grows, so market rent rises about 3% a year: $2,000 a month today.
  • New law: for tenants already living in a flat, rent may rise at most 2% a year.
  • When a tenant moves out, the flat's rent resets to the market rent.
  • Landlords may stop renting: sell the flat to an owner-occupier or redevelop it.
