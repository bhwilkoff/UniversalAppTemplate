Checking balance, then searching for it

The t-test sees only means, so it misses differences in spread and shape. The KS test sees the whole distribution. GenMatch uses both.

The exception is a binary variable: its whole distribution is the share of 1s, which is also its mean. Both tests then ask the same question, and in large samples their p-values coincide.

So: if these tests measure balance, do you want their p-values as high as possible or as low as possible?
