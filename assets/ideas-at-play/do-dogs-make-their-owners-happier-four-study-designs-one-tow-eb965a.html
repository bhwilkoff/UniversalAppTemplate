Do dogs make their owners happier?

H: Dogs cause an increase in their owner's happiness because of the positive psychological effects of the relationship.

This town has 400 people, and some of them own a dog. The question is about owning one: having a dog of your own, living with it and caring for it. Being near a dog doesn't count: petting a stranger's dog, passing one in the park or walking one for money is not the same as owning it.

The town has a hidden answer: here, owning a dog raises a person's happiness by nothing, a little or a lot. You will run four studies and decide which ones you can trust. As you go, ask whether each study measures what owning a dog does, or only what being near one does.
