Opening the board…

The board is for the people in a cohort.

Sign in with the GitHub account you joined with, and this page will open the board your cohort draws on together during the session.

Sign in with GitHub

This board is for the people in its cohort.

If you are in it, open the board from the session page, which you can reach from your account.

The session page

Everyone in the cohort sees what you draw here as you draw it, and the board is saved every few seconds and when you leave, so it is still here after the session. It is deleted with the rest of the session when the cohort finishes, so download anything you want to keep. Pictures cannot be added, only drawing and writing.

The board is Excalidraw, which is open source under the MIT License.

Clear the board for everyone?

Everything on it disappears from every screen and from what is saved, and it cannot be brought back. If anyone might want it, download it first.
