This board is for the people in its cohort.

If you are in it, open the board from the session page, which you can reach from your account.

The session page

Everyone in the cohort sees what you draw here as you draw it, and the board is saved every few seconds and when you leave, so it is still here after the session. It is deleted with the rest of the session when the cohort finishes, so download anything you want to keep. You can add a PDF or pictures as pages to draw on, and a teacher can lock the board so only they can add or draw.

The board is Excalidraw, which is open source under the MIT License.

Clear the board for everyone?

Everything on it disappears from every screen and from what is saved, and it cannot be brought back. If anyone might want it, download it first.
